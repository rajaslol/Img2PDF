How to Use Snap2PDF

1

Add your images

Click Select Images or drag and drop JPG, PNG, or WebP files onto the upload area. Each image becomes a separate page in the PDF. You can add multiple images at once.

2

Choose your settings

Select a page orientation (Portrait or Landscape), a page size (A4, US Letter, or match the original image), and a margin. Enable compression and adjust the quality level if you want a smaller file size.

3

Name your PDF

Enter a filename in the PDF Filename field. The .pdf extension is added automatically if you skip it.

4

Convert and download

Click the purple Convert to PDF button. The PDF is built entirely inside your browser and saved straight to your device — no server, no waiting.

5

Removing an image

Hover over any thumbnail and click the × icon in the corner to remove it before converting.

6

Works offline too

Once the page has loaded, you can disconnect from the internet and conversion still works. All processing is done locally with JavaScript.
